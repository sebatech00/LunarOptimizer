Before you start

A few things to know.

Does Lunar require root?

No. Advanced operations use Shizuku's Android shell access. Android can still reject individual operations; check the result shown in the app.

Will it work on my phone?

Android 11+ is the minimum. Xiaomi 14 with HyperOS 3 is the primary target. Other devices and firmware versions are unverified, and system output can differ.

Does it guarantee better battery life or smoother animations?

No. Those are goals that require controlled device measurements. The general frame/jank measurement pipeline remains unfinished. The project documents implemented mechanisms separately from measured benefits.

Can every action be undone?

No. The journal supports restoration of recorded settings and eligible package state. It cannot recover deleted files or cleared caches. Clearing Lunar's data or uninstalling it erases its original-value records.

What has been tested?

The supplied files passed ZIP integrity checks, and the APK passed the included DEX check. There are 61 unit test annotations in source; those tests were not rerun for this publication. No phone installation or performance validation was performed here.

Is the project open source?

The source is available to inspect, but an application-wide license has not yet been selected. Third-party libraries retain their own licenses. See the repository's notices for details.
